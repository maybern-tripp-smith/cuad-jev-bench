Contract-clause relevance on CUAD: pairwise Choice and graded Score under a frozen criterion

Overview Analysis Report GitHub

Abstract

Human annotations of contract clauses are a natural but incomplete label for relevance to a requested legal category. Nearby paragraphs often share vocabulary without being on-point.

This note reports a pre-registered evaluation of TypeSafe/Jev on the open CUAD corpus under the fixed criterion more relevant to the requested contract category. Primary quantities are reported with standard errors (s.e.). Gate 4 is a construct-validity contrast: mean Score on gold spans versus BM25 hard-negatives under the same category query. Gate 7 cites published DeBERTa extractive metrics for difficulty context only and does not re-run DeBERTa.

Measurement

Two constructs are distinguished. The annotation measure is recovery of human CUAD spans. The relevance measure is pairwise Choice and graded Score against hard negatives under a fixed category description.

Gates 1, 3 (signal), 4, and 6 are pre-registered pass/fail or signal tests. Gates 2, 5, and 7 are report-only.
